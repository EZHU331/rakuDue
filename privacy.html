Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 September 2026 · App: Raku (com.raku.app)

Raku (“we”, “the app”) is an independent iOS app. This policy explains how Raku handles information. Raku is designed so that habit and spend processing happens on your device.

1. Summary

  • No bank account login.
  • Mail and Wallet data you allow are read and processed on-device.
  • Optional iCloud sync keeps your Raku data with your Apple ID across your devices.
  • We do not sell your personal or financial data.

2. Information the app may process

Depending on features you use, Raku may process:

  • Habits you create — names, goals, check-ins, and related settings.
  • Spend trail — merchants, amounts, dates, and categories from sources you connect or import (for example statement dumps, pasted receipts, iCloud Mail receipts, or Apple Wallet transactions if you grant access).
  • Recurring signals — subscription-like items you save or that are inferred from your trail (such as iCloud+ or Apple Media charges appearing in that trail).
  • Health data you allow — steps and mindful minutes via Apple Health, used only for habit goals you enable.
  • Screen Time selection you allow — opaque app/category tokens for screen-limit goals; Raku does not receive app content.
  • Display preferences — such as preferred currency and optional display name on the home LCD.

3. iCloud Mail (IMAP)

If you connect iCloud Mail, Raku uses IMAP (imap.mail.me.com) with an app-specific password you provide. Credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. Messages are fetched and parsed on-device to find receipt-like spend signals. Mail content is not uploaded to our servers — Raku does not operate a mail backend for this feature.

4. Apple Wallet / FinanceKit

If Apple grants FinanceKit access and you authorize it, Raku may read Apple Card, Apple Cash, and related Wallet account transactions you allow, on-device, to update your spend trail and habit pulse. Access requires your explicit permission and can be revoked in iOS Settings.

5. iCloud sync

When you are signed into iCloud, Raku may sync app data (habits, check-ins, spend events, recurring items) through Apple CloudKit under container iCloud.com.raku.app. That sync is between your devices and Apple’s iCloud for your account — not a separate Raku cloud database we operate.

6. On-device intelligence

Optional narrative polish may use Apple’s on-device Foundation Models / Apple Intelligence when available. Prompts and results for this feature are intended to remain on-device; they are not used to train a Raku cloud model.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not require a bank connection or open-banking login for core use.
  • We do not sell personal data or financial transaction data.
  • We do not use your spend trail for third-party advertising.

8. Data retention and deletion

Data lives on your device and, if enabled, in your iCloud account for the app. You can remove connected mail credentials in the app, revoke Health, Screen Time, and Wallet permissions in iOS Settings, and delete the app (and related iCloud data for the app via Apple’s iCloud management) to remove local Raku data.

9. Children

Raku is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). Do not use the app to submit personal data of children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The effective date above will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised through the App Store product page for Raku.
